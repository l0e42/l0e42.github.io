About

I am interested in

Since October 2024 I am an assistant professor at the Department of Computer Science at Aalborg University! I am a member of the DEIS group. Before this, from October 2022, I was a postdoc at the Department of Information Technology at Uppsala University. I worked mostly with Parosh Abdulla and the algorithmic program verification group. I finished my PhD at Reykjavik University in October 2022, under the supervision of Luca Aceto and Anna Ingolfsdottir. Before Iceland, I lived in Greece, where I finished my masters in applied mathematics in National Technical University of Athens under the supervision of professors Aris Pagourtzis and Stathis Zachos.
